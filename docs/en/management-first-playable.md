# The first playable scene of a management game

Decide where the player arrives, what they can choose, and which object in the scene will carry the result. In Riverside Homestead a family waits on the bank and moves into the home built on the player's chosen site. Selection, preview, construction and arrival stay in one camera view. When adapting it, find characters and consequences that belong to the new setting.

## From one sentence to a first draft

1. Pick a verb and the object it changes. Say who is waiting, what is missing, and whose behavior will change. Make that situation legible on entry.
2. Arrange two or three possible sites or subjects. Use silhouettes, a character's gaze or markers in the scene to lead the eye. Preview at the selected location. Cancellation is free and confirmation spends the resource once.
3. Give the action a beginning, a middle and a consequence. Raising the frame, fitting the roof and walking to the door are three visible results of one decision. Silhouettes and state changes should still communicate the result with sound muted or motion reduced.
4. Keep selection, cost and settled state in the simulation. Keyboard, click and touch should commit the same action. Preserve saves and exit behavior. Keep settings, language and text alternatives accessible from the scene. Build the first action before expanding the game's other systems.

To change genres, adapt the beneficiary, the resource, the props, the player's verb and the behavior that follows completion. Preserve sprite foot points and transparent edges when replacing preview, construction and final art. The asset notes document dimensions, placement and licensing.

Before adding a permanent side panel, try the opening with the panel folded away. Put the next action beside the person or plot it affects. If this still needs a checklist to be understandable, change the scene or the on-site cue. Reuse suitable local sprites before spending the edit budget generating new scenery.

## Observe the unpolished draft

Preserve the model's first files and roughly twenty seconds from opening through the first interaction. Invite someone new to play without coaching. Afterwards ask who they helped, which part of the world they changed and what happened. Record the first voluntary action, any help required and any unclear consequence. Browser checks can verify spending and pixels. A player's interpretation needs their own response.

From the repository root run `npm run create -- MyVillage --template management --title "溪边小镇 · Riverside Town"`. The generated directory includes its own engine copy, art, both authoring guides and tests.
