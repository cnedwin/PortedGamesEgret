# PortedGamesEgret

基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)开发的网页游戏 Demo 集合。每个游戏位于自己的目录，独立运行和维护。 [进入展示站](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/)

A collection of browser game demos built with [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild). Each game lives in its own directory and can be run and maintained independently. [Visit the showcase](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/)

## FivePhases · 五行牌阵

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 2D 卡牌 + 三消混合游戏 Demo。上半屏是带立绘动效的卡牌回合对战，下半屏是消除盘：消掉什么颜色就攒什么能量，卡牌按自己的元素收费；消除本身还会掉新卡，而卡与卡之间的羁绊（剑盾同源、三昧、残响、连锁体质、星轨）反过来决定你该怎么消。五行既是消除的配色与五种形状，也是五个流派——火爆发、土护盾反击、金破甲暴击、水控场改盘、木持续成长，相生让下一张牌伤害 +50%，相克只作用于伤害、绝不改变棋盘。三关递进，关间奖励三选一。立绘是用代码按 Spine 槽位结构画出的占位件，骨骼、层序、轴心定死后只差填图。支持鼠标与触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FivePhases/) · [说明与开发](FivePhases/README.md)

An independently developed 2D card battler over a match-three board, built with Egret Rebuild. The top half is turn-based card combat with animated portraits; the bottom half is the board: whatever colour you clear is the energy you gain, and every card is paid for in its own element. Clearing also drops new cards, and the bonds between them (sword and shield, samadhi, echo, chain body, star lane) decide how you should be matching. The five phases are at once the board's colours and five shapes, and five playstyles — fire bursts, earth shields and counters, metal pierces, water rearranges the board, wood grows — with the feeding cycle adding 50% to the next card and the overcoming cycle touching damage only, never the board. Three stages, with a pick-one-of-three reward between them. The portraits are placeholder pieces drawn in code to Spine's slot structure, with bones, layers and pivots fixed so that only the images are left to fill in. Mouse and touch. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FivePhases/) · [Guide and development](FivePhases/README.md)

An independently developed rhythm game demo of the Peking opera percussion, built with Egret Rebuild. You take the drummer's seat: the syllables of the percussion score (cang, cai, tai, ba, da, du) become seals and diamonds flowing towards the drum face, and you strike the drum or the gongs as they arrive; a big cang wants both hands for double points, and a long du is a roll. Three songs, The General Arms, Riding Through and Swift Wind, are made of common patterns of the traditional score, each on easy, normal and hard, over the clapper's beat and a jinghu fiddle in the bridges. The drum, gongs, cymbals, clapper and fiddle are all synthesized in code and played through the engine's mixer, and the song's time is the backing voice's position less the output delay, with a delay measure by striking along with the clapper. Chinese and English, keyboard, touch, mouse and gamepad. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/OperaDrums/) · [Guide and development](OperaDrums/README.md)

## Taiwan Sixteen-Tile Mahjong · 台灣十六張麻將

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的台湾十六张麻将游戏 Demo。玩家和三位电脑牌友同桌：稳健的阿德、急攻的小美、爱做大牌的老陈，程度分入门、普通、高手。补花、吃碰槓、截胡、抢槓、流局连庄都按台湾十六张的打法，按常见台数表算台、底加台结算，结算面板逐项列出台数。有听牌提示、托管、战绩和存档；界面繁体中文和英文，键盘、触屏、鼠标都能玩，竖着拿手机时牌桌自动转向。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/TaiwanMahjong/) · [说明与开发](TaiwanMahjong/README.md)

An independently developed demo of Taiwanese sixteen-tile mahjong, built with Egret Rebuild. The player sits with three computer players — steady Ah-De, eager Mei and Old Chen who chases big hands — at three levels. Flowers, chows, pongs and kongs, the nearest winner on a discard, robbing a kong and the dealer keeping the deal all follow Taiwanese play, with a common tai table, base-plus-tai payment and every tai listed at the end of a hand. Ready hints, auto-play, a record and a save; traditional Chinese and English, keyboard, touch and mouse, and the table turns itself on an upright phone.
