# PortedGamesEgret

基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)开发的网页游戏 Demo 集合。每个游戏位于自己的目录，独立运行和维护。 [进入展示站](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/)

A collection of browser game demos built with [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild). Each game lives in its own directory and can be run and maintained independently. [Visit the showcase](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/)

## JY · 金庸群侠传致敬 Demo

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的武侠网页游戏 Demo。玩家可以探索江湖、与人物交谈、招募队友并组队出战。作品向《金庸群侠传》、智冠公司与金庸先生致敬。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/JY/) · [完整版使用说明](JY/README.md)

An independently developed wuxia browser game demo built with Egret Rebuild. Explore the martial world, meet its characters, recruit companions, and take your party into battle. This demo pays tribute to *Heroes of Jin Yong*, Soft-World, and Jin Yong. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/JY/) · [Full game guide](JY/README.md)

打开试玩页即可进入经典回合制完整版，首次启动自动加载已获授权的原版运行数据。

Play the full classic turn-based game in your browser. The first launch automatically loads the original runtime data authorized for this demo.

## Jelly Pandas · 果冻熊猫

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 3D 动作生存游戏 Demo。半透明的果冻熊猫要在玩具岛上抵挡糖果大军十五分钟：十二种自动开火的玩具可以升级和进化，还能用大跳、下砸、肚皮撞和翻滚把敌人撞飞，每五分钟迎战一个 Boss。可单人游玩，也可带上由游戏操控的伙伴。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/jelly-pandas/) · [说明与开发](jelly-pandas/README.md)

An independently developed 3D action survival demo built with Egret Rebuild. See-through jelly pandas hold a toy island for fifteen minutes against a candy horde: twelve self-firing toys level up and evolve, super jumps, slams, belly bumps and rolls send enemies flying, and a boss arrives every five minutes. Play alone or with friends steered by the game. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/jelly-pandas/) · [Guide and development](jelly-pandas/README.md)

## Jinxiu Rally · 锦绣飞车

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 3D 赛车游戏 Demo。醒狮、熊猫、美猴王、玉兔、青龙、秦俑、京剧花脸和虎头娃八位车手，在有牌楼、石桥、宝塔和长城的“山水环道”上竞速：漂移攒出加速，捡铜钱、穿祥云门、借尾流，绕开泥潭、石狮和滚鼓，再用爆竹、蹴鞠、纸鸢、金钟罩、雷公鼓、筋斗云等十二种道具争夺名次。三个级别，可选 1、3 或 5 圈，支持键盘、手柄和触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/Rally/) · [说明与开发](Rally/README.md)

An independently developed 3D kart racing demo built with Egret Rebuild. Eight racers (a lion dancer, a panda, the Monkey King, the Jade Rabbit, the Azure Dragon, a terracotta warrior, a Peking opera mask and a child in a tiger hat) race the Mountain and Water Circuit past a ceremonial gate, a stone bridge, pagodas and the long wall: drift for boosts, collect coins, thread cloud gates and use slipstreams, steer round mud, stone lions and rolling drums, and fight for places with twelve items, from firecrackers, cuju balls and kites to a golden bell, a thunder drum and a somersault cloud. Three classes over 1, 3 or 5 laps, with keyboard, gamepad and touch controls. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/Rally/) · [Guide and development](Rally/README.md)

## Love Elective · 心动选修课

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 2D 校园恋爱养成游戏 Demo。大一新生选了一门只有一份作业的选修课：三十天后的星空祭之前，真正认识一个人，并把心意告诉她。七位性格各异的学姐各有四章带分支的故事和两种结局；每天分四段时间去上课、训练、打工、做任务、买礼物，摸清每个人喜欢的相处距离。衣橱里有四十件服饰，每件都能自由染色，也能为学姐搭配。分层视差的场景带来伪 2.5D 的纵深感，支持中英文、鼠标和触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LoveElective/) · [说明与开发](LoveElective/README.md)

An independently developed 2D campus romance and dress-up demo built with Egret Rebuild. A first-year student takes an elective with a single assignment: before the Star Festival thirty days away, truly get to know one person and tell her how you feel. Seven seniors with very different personalities each have four branching story chapters and two endings; every day has four parts for classes, training, part-time work, tasks and presents, and for learning how close each of them likes you to stand. The wardrobe holds forty pieces, each freely dyeable, and the seniors can be dressed too. Layered parallax scenes give a pseudo-2.5D sense of depth, with Chinese and English text and mouse or touch controls. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LoveElective/) · [Guide and development](LoveElective/README.md)

## Wulin · 武林天下

一款为新白鹭引擎独立开发的武侠门派经营游戏 Demo。从十二个门派里择一而掌：建山门、收弟子、排差事、练武学，与各派或战或和，直到一统江湖或被推为武林盟主。目前为开发中的版本，规则、数值、存档和修改器已可用，画面暂为文字面板，引擎绘制的 3D 场景还在制作。 [试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/Wulin/) · [说明](Wulin/README.md)

An independently developed wuxia sect management demo being built for Egret Rebuild. Take charge of one of twelve sects: build its seat, take in and train disciples, and fight or befriend the other sects until you rule the martial world or are named its leader. A work in progress: the rules, numbers, saves and editor work, the game is shown as text panels for now, and the 3D scenes drawn with the engine are still being made. [Play](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/Wulin/) · [Guide](Wulin/README.md)

## Operation Cutoff · 断流行动

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的第一人称战术射击游戏 Demo。黎明时分，特勤干员潜入被武装分子占据的水电站：在堆场、厂房和办公楼找到三处数据终端并下载情报，搜刮按稀有度分色的物资，用步枪、手枪和手雷顶住步枪兵、突击兵和重装兵的一波波增援，最后赶到停机坪登上直升机撤离。场景、角色、贴图和音效全部由代码生成，支持中英文、键鼠和触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FPS/) · [说明与开发](FPS/README.md)

An independently developed first-person tactical shooter demo built with Egret Rebuild. At first light an operator slips into a hydropower station held by an armed militia: download the three data terminals in the cargo yard, the powerhouse and the office block, search crates for loot coloured by rarity, hold off waves of riflemen, assaulters and heavies with a rifle, a pistol and grenades, then reach the helipad and board the helicopter. The station, the soldiers, the textures and the sound are all generated in code, with Chinese and English text and keyboard, mouse or touch controls. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FPS/) · [Guide and development](FPS/README.md)

## Lantern Shadows · 灯影·闹天宫

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 2D 横版动作游戏 Demo，整出戏演在皮影戏的幕布上：灯在幕后，镂空上色的皮偶贴着幕布，孙悟空一路打上南天门。皮偶是 Spine 骨骼动画，每片皮的轮廓与花纹都由代码画出，关卡是一张瓦片地图；平扫、上挑、劈砸三下连击各有一记锣鼓，打中时整台戏定格、震动。第一幕有十名天兵把守：枪兵出枪前有预警，弓手的箭可以一棍打落，筋斗云能从枪尖里穿过去。南天门前哪吒踏风火轮拦路，分三段打：火尖枪、能一棍打回去的乾坤圈、三头六臂与烧过地面的风火轮。后面的两幕还在做。支持键盘、手柄和触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LanternShadows/) · [说明与开发](LanternShadows/README.md)

An independently developed 2D side-scrolling action demo built with Egret Rebuild, played as a shadow-puppet show: a lamp behind a cloth, carved and dyed leather puppets against it, and the Monkey King fighting his way to the Southern Gate of Heaven. The puppets are Spine skeletons whose every piece is drawn by code, and the level is a tile map; the sweep, the lift and the smash of his combination each have their beat of percussion, and the stage holds still and shudders when a blow lands. Ten soldiers of heaven hold the first act: a spearman warns before he thrusts, an archer’s arrow can be knocked out of the air, and the cloud roll passes through a spear point. Before the gate Nezha comes down on his wheels of wind and fire and fights in three phases: the fire-tipped spear, the Universe Ring that the staff can send back at him, and three heads and six arms on wheels that leave the ground burning. Two more acts are still to come. Keyboard, gamepad and touch. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LanternShadows/) · [Guide and development](LanternShadows/README.md)
