A collection of browser game demos built with [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild). Each game lives in its own directory and can be run and maintained independently. [Visit the showcase](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/)

## FivePhases · 五行牌阵

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的 2D 卡牌 + 三消混合游戏 Demo。上半屏是带立绘动效的卡牌回合对战，下半屏是消除盘：消掉什么颜色就攒什么能量，卡牌按自己的元素收费；消除本身还会掉新卡，而卡与卡之间的羁绊（剑盾同源、三昧、残响、连锁体质、星轨）反过来决定你该怎么消。五行既是消除的配色与五种形状，也是五个流派——火爆发、土护盾反击、金破甲暴击、水控场改盘、木持续成长，相生让下一张牌伤害 +50%，相克只作用于伤害、绝不改变棋盘。三关递进，关间奖励三选一。立绘是用代码按 Spine 槽位结构画出的占位件，骨骼、层序、轴心定死后只差填图。支持鼠标与触屏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FivePhases/) · [说明与开发](FivePhases/README.md)

An independently developed 2D card battler over a match-three board, built with Egret Rebuild. The top half is turn-based card combat with animated portraits; the bottom half is the board: whatever colour you clear is the energy you gain, and every card is paid for in its own element. Clearing also drops new cards, and the bonds between them (sword and shield, samadhi, echo, chain body, star lane) decide how you should be matching. The five phases are at once the board's colours and five shapes, and five playstyles — fire bursts, earth shields and counters, metal pierces, water rearranges the board, wood grows — with the feeding cycle adding 50% to the next card and the overcoming cycle touching damage only, never the board. Three stages, with a pick-one-of-three reward between them. The portraits are placeholder pieces drawn in code to Spine's slot structure, with bones, layers and pivots fixed so that only the images are left to fill in. Mouse and touch. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/FivePhases/) · [Guide and development](FivePhases/README.md)

## Opera Drums · 锣鼓经

一款使用新白鹭引擎独立开发的京剧锣鼓节奏游戏 Demo。你坐在鼓师的位置上：锣鼓经里的「仓、才、台、八、大、嘟」变成方印和菱形，从右边流向鼓面，到达时敲鼓或打锣；大的「仓」两只手一起敲得分加倍，长条的「嘟」是滚奏。三首曲子《起霸》《趟马》《急急风》由传统锣鼓经的常用点子拼成，各有易、中、难三档，伴奏是板眼和过门里的京胡。单皮鼓、小锣、大锣、铙钹、板和京胡的声音全部在代码里合成，交给引擎的混音器播放，曲子的时间取自伴奏声部的位置并扣除输出延迟；还有跟着板声敲的延迟校准。中英文，键盘、触屏、鼠标和手柄都能玩。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/OperaDrums/) · [说明与开发](OperaDrums/README.md)

An independently developed rhythm game demo of the Peking opera percussion, built with Egret Rebuild. You take the drummer's seat: the syllables of the percussion score (cang, cai, tai, ba, da, du) become seals and diamonds flowing towards the drum face, and you strike the drum or the gongs as they arrive; a big cang wants both hands for double points, and a long du is a roll. Three songs, The General Arms, Riding Through and Swift Wind, are made of common patterns of the traditional score, each on easy, normal and hard, over the clapper's beat and a jinghu fiddle in the bridges. The drum, gongs, cymbals, clapper and fiddle are all synthesized in code and played through the engine's mixer, and the song's time is the backing voice's position less the output delay, with a delay measure by striking along with the clapper. 一款基于新白鹭引擎独立开发的 2D 物理造桥解谜游戏 Demo。取自民间“赵州桥，鲁班修”的传说：你是鲁班门下的匠人，走遍江南、赵州、蜀道、潮州和侗乡，在网格上搭木梁、竹架、石拱、悬索和浮桥，让挑夫、花轿、马车和运石车过河。点“试通”后桥身按真实受力弯曲、变色，太吃力的杆件会拉断、压坏、掉进河里；每根杆件的力由小步长的位置约束直接算出，车轮、碎木和桥面刚体交给引擎的 2D 物理。五章二十五关，每章最后重建一座名桥（赵州桥、泸定桥、广济桥、程阳风雨桥），三枚印章（过桥、省钱、无损），另有不限预算的沙盒。原创青绿水彩封面与五章远景、透明树木结合引擎实时结构绘制，配乐与材料拟音由代码原创合成。中英文，横屏竖屏，鼠标、触屏、键盘、手柄都能玩，也能导出为微信小游戏。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LubanBridge/) · [说明与开发](LubanBridge/README.md)

An independently developed 2D physics bridge-building puzzle demo built with Egret Rebuild. It takes the folk saying that the carpenter Lu Ban built the Zhaozhou Bridge: you are one of his apprentices, travelling through Jiangnan, Zhaozhou, the Shu Road, Chaozhou and Dong country, laying timber beams, bamboo frames, stone arches, suspension cables and pontoon bridges on a grid so that porters, a bridal sedan, horse carts and a stone wagon can cross. On Test the bridge bends and colours with its real loads, and a member that works too hard tears or crushes and falls into the river; each member's force comes straight out of small-step position constraints, and the wheels, debris and deck bodies are the engine's 2D physics. Five chapters of five levels, each ending with a famous bridge built again (Zhaozhou, Luding, Guangji, Chengyang), three seals (across, thrift, unbroken), and a sandbox without a budget. Original watercolor cover, five regional plates and transparent trees complement real-time structural drawing. The original music and material foley are synthesized in code. Chinese and English, wide and tall, mouse, touch, keyboard and gamepad, and an export as a WeChat mini game.
