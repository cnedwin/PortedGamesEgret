# Love Elective · 心动选修课

心动选修课是基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)独立开发的 2D 校园恋爱养成游戏 Demo。你是白鹭学院的大一新生，选了一门只有一份作业的选修课：在三十天后的星空祭之前，真正认识一个人，并把心意告诉她。七位性格各异的学姐分布在校园各处，每人有四章带分支的故事；你要安排每天的四段时间去上课、训练、打工、做任务、买礼物和换装，摸清每个人喜欢的相处距离。画面用分层视差做出纵深感。 [在线试玩](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LoveElective/)

Love Elective is an independently developed 2D campus romance and dress-up demo built with [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild). You are a first-year student at Egret Academy who signs up for an elective with a single assignment: before the Star Festival thirty days from now, truly get to know one person and tell her how you feel. Seven seniors with very different personalities are spread across the campus, each with four branching story chapters. You plan the four parts of every day around classes, training, part-time work, tasks, presents and outfits, and learn how close each of them likes you to stand. Layered parallax gives the flat pictures depth. [Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/LoveElective/)

游戏中的角色均为成年大学生。 All characters are adult university students.

## 玩法 · How to play

| | 操作 · Controls |
|---|---|
| 选择、确认 · Choose and confirm | 鼠标点击或触屏轻点 · click or tap |
| 继续对话 · Continue dialogue | 点击、空格或回车 · click, Space or Enter |
| 选择选项 · Pick an option | 点击，或数字键 1–3 · click, or keys 1–3 |
| 跳过剧情 · Skip a scene | 对话框右上角“跳过”，或 Ctrl · the Skip button on the dialogue box, or Ctrl |
| 关闭弹窗 · Close a pop-up | Esc，或点“关闭” · Esc, or the Close button |

- 每天分为上午、下午、傍晚、夜晚。在校园地图上选地点，在那里做一件事会用掉一段时间；教学楼、图书馆等地方夜间关闭。
- 体力不够时回宿舍小憩或睡觉。第三十天夜晚过后进入星空祭。
- 游戏会在每次行动后自动保存到当前浏览器，标题界面可以继续。

- A day has a morning, afternoon, evening and night. Pick a place on the campus map; doing something there uses one part of the day. The lecture hall, library and some other places close at night.
- When energy runs low, nap or sleep in the dormitory. The Star Festival begins after the night of day thirty.
- The game saves to the browser after every action, and can be continued from the title screen.

每位学姐的四章故事在她常在的地点发生：第一次相遇是第一章，之后的章节需要足够的好感和她看重的属性。地图上的金色星星表示有新的故事。手机里可以查看每个人现在在哪、下一章还差什么。

Each senior's four chapters take place where she usually is. The first meeting is chapter one; later chapters need enough affection and enough of the stat she respects. A gold star on the map marks a new story. The phone shows where everyone is and what the next chapter still needs.

### 好感与拉扯 · Affection and push-and-pull

- **聊天**：每段时间可以和在场的学姐聊一次，并选择这次的距离：靠近一步、自然相处或留点余白。每个人喜欢的距离不同——有人喜欢你主动，有人需要空间。聊过几次后，她觉得舒服的范围会显示在“拉扯”条上；落在范围内好感涨得最快，太急或太疏远都会适得其反。
- **礼物**：每人每天可以送一次。喜好各不相同，送错了会扣好感。
- **约会**：好感达到 35 后可以邀约，会用掉一段时间。被其他好感较高的学姐撞见，她会难过。
- **穿搭**：穿成她偏爱的风格，聊天和约会的好感加成更高。

- **Chat:** once per part of the day with a senior who is present, choosing your distance: step closer, stay easy, or give space. Each of them likes a different distance; some want you forward, some need room. After a few talks her comfortable range appears on the push-and-pull bar. Inside it affection grows fastest; rushing her or staying aloof backfires.
- **Presents:** one a day for each senior. Tastes differ, and a bad choice costs affection.
- **Dates:** from 35 affection, and they use a part of the day. If another senior who is fond of you sees it, she is hurt.
- **Outfits:** dress in a style she favours and chats and dates count for more.

### 换装 · Dressing up

宿舍的衣橱里可以更换上装、下装、外套、鞋子、袜子、饰品和发型，共四十件服饰和五种发型。穿着中的每一件都有主色和辅色两个染色通道：可以点选色板，也可以在色带上调出任意颜色。发色同样可以更改。每套穿搭会按清爽、学院、运动、街头、文艺、优雅、可爱、酷感八种风格计分，并提供魅力加成。

商业街的花漾衣橱出售服饰。其中的裙装等女装可以买来送给学姐，收下后会进入她的衣橱；好感达到 35，她就愿意让你帮她搭配和染色，更高的好感会解锁她的日常装和约会装。

The dormitory wardrobe changes tops, bottoms, outerwear, shoes, legwear, accessories and hairstyle: forty pieces of clothing and five hairstyles. Everything worn has two dye channels, a main and a trim colour: pick a swatch, or tap the colour strip for any shade. Hair colour can be changed too. Each outfit scores in eight styles (Fresh, Preppy, Sporty, Street, Artsy, Elegant, Cute, Cool) and adds to Charm.

Bloom Boutique on the shopping street sells clothes. Dresses, skirts and other pieces for the seniors can be bought as presents and join the wardrobe of whoever receives them. From 35 affection a senior lets you choose and dye her outfit, and higher affection unlocks her casual and date looks.

### 属性、打工与任务 · Stats, work and tasks

| 属性 · Stat | 提升方式 · How to raise it |
|---|---|
| 学识 · Intellect | 教学楼听课、图书馆自习、机房外包 · lectures, studying in the library, coding jobs |
| 体能 · Fitness | 操场训练、发传单 · running laps, handing out flyers |
| 才艺 · Art | 美术室素描、音乐室练歌 · sketching, vocal practice |
| 魅力 · Charm | 音乐室练歌、咖啡店打工、穿搭 · vocal practice, café shifts, outfits |
| 情商 · Empathy | 天台发呆、听课、咖啡店打工 · watching the sky, lectures, café shifts |

打工赚到的钱可以买服饰、礼物和补给（能量饮料与提升属性的书）。校门的公告栏和手机里有二十多个任务：新生报到、文艺汇演、校运会、期末考、风格穿搭挑战，以及每位学姐的委托，完成后领取金钱、属性或好感。

Money from work buys clothes, presents and supplies (energy drinks and books that raise stats). The notice board at the gate and the phone list more than twenty tasks: finding your way around, the arts showcase, sports day, final exams, outfit challenges and a request from each senior, rewarded with money, stats or affection.

### 结局 · Endings

星空祭之夜可以向好感达到 55 的学姐告白。读完她的全部四章且好感达到 88，会进入她的恋爱结局；否则是“未完待续”。也可以谁都不说。每位学姐各有两种结局，另有一个独自的结局。

On the night of the Star Festival you may confess to any senior at 55 affection or more. With all four of her chapters read and 88 affection, you reach her love ending; otherwise the answer waits for another term. You may also tell no one. Each senior has two endings, and there is one for going alone.

## 美术与画面 · Art direction

封面、九位角色的剧情立绘、校园地图、场景、前景装饰和商品图采用为本 Demo 生成的插画。对白使用角色的默认造型插画。换装预览将独立绘制的角色头部与衣服、配饰分层组合，染色和换发型也能即时显示。场景把背景、人物和前景放在不同深度，保留昼夜色调与视差。

The cover, nine character portraits, campus map, locations, foreground props, and catalogue art were generated for this demo. Dialogue uses the illustrated default looks. The dress-up system layers illustrated heads with clothing and accessories and updates dyes and hairstyles live. Background, characters, and foreground sit on different depth planes with parallax and time-of-day lighting.

手机使用横屏可完整观看舞台式界面。

On phones, landscape gives the stage layout its full viewing area.

## 引擎用法 · How it uses the engine

游戏使用白鹭的共享帧 2D 配置，默认以 WebGL2 绘制。各部分对应的引擎功能如下。

The game uses Egret's shared-frame 2D profile and draws with WebGL2 by default. Each part maps to an engine feature as follows.

| 部分 · Part | 引擎功能 · Engine feature |
|---|---|
| 启动与每帧提交 · Start-up and frame submission | `createEngineForProfile(…, 'egret.shared-frame2d/1')`，`createSharedWebGL2Host` 或 `createSharedWebGPUHost`，`renderSharedFrame`；上一帧完成后才提交下一帧 · the next frame is submitted only when the host has finished the last |
| 立绘、背景、界面的贴图 · Textures for characters, backgrounds and interface | 画布绘制的像素经 `createImageData2D`、`createTexture` 成为贴图，由 `engine.assets` 以租约管理，换图时释放旧租约 · canvas pixels become textures, held as leases through `engine.assets` and released when a picture is replaced |
| 画面层次 · Display tree | `DisplayObjectContainer`、`Bitmap`、`Sprite` 的位置、缩放、旋转、透明度和锚点 · position, scale, rotation, alpha and anchors |
| 伪 2.5D 纵深 · Pseudo-2.5D depth | 背景、角色、近景和粒子分层，以不同幅度平移并整体推近。地图与地点标签分别移动 · background, character, foreground and particle planes shift by different amounts and zoom together. The map and its pins move independently |
| 昼夜光线 · Time-of-day light | `createSharedMaterial2D` 的线性光色为角色染上黄昏或夜色，并统一使用线性过滤 · the light colour of a shared material tints characters for dusk and night, with linear filtering throughout |
| 对话逐字显示 · Text writing itself out | 同一张文字贴图放进两个带 `clipRect` 的容器：一个显示已完成的行，一个显示正在出现的行 · one text texture in two containers with `clipRect`: finished lines in one, the growing line in the other |
| 眨眼 · Blinking | 分层换装人物使用独立的眨眼贴图 · layered dress-up figures use a separate blink texture |
| 点击判定 · Tap detection | `globalToLocal`、`localToGlobal` 把指针换算到面板坐标 · convert the pointer into panel coordinates |
| 动画与计时 · Animation and timing | `engine.advanceTime` 驱动 `engine.clock.onTick`，补间、粒子和打字效果都挂在这一个时钟上 · tweens, particles and text all run from this one clock |
| 淡入淡出、悬停高亮 · Fades and hover highlight | `Sprite.graphics` 的纯色矩形 · solid rectangles |

音乐和音效由 Web Audio 合成。时间、属性、好感、拉扯、任务和结局规则可脱离页面运行，在 Node 中玩完整个学期。

Music and sound are synthesised with Web Audio. The time, stats, affection, push-and-pull, task and ending rules can run a whole term in Node without a page.

## 运行与开发 · Run and develop

游戏是纯静态页面，无需构建。在本目录启动任意静态文件服务器，然后打开显示的地址：

The game is a static page with no build step. Start any static file server in this folder and open the address it prints:

```sh
npx serve .
```

地址参数 · Address options：

| 参数 · Option | 作用 · Effect |
|---|---|
| `?lang=zh` / `?lang=en` | 界面与剧情语言，也可在标题和手机里切换 · language of interface and story; it can also be switched on the title screen and in the phone |
| `?backend=webgpu` | 使用 WebGPU 绘制；无法启动时自动改用 WebGL2 · draw with WebGPU; if it cannot start, the game switches to WebGL2 |
| `?ratio=1` … `?ratio=2` | 画面像素比，默认随设备、最高 1.5 · pixel ratio of the picture; by default it follows the device, up to 1.5 |

### 测试 · Tests

使用 Node.js 22 或更新版本，在本目录运行：

Use Node.js 22 or later and run from this folder:

```sh
node --test
```

不开页面玩完一个学期：按给定的学姐安排每天的行动，输出各章故事的进度和最终结局，适合检查规则和数值改动。

Play a whole term without a page: the tool plans each day around the seniors you name and prints story progress and the ending, which suits checking changes to rules and numbers.

```sh
node tools/headless.mjs wanqing
node tools/headless.mjs qingge bailu
```
