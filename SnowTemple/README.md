# Snow at the Mountain Temple · 风雪山神庙

风雪山神庙是基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)独立开发的俯视角 3D 动作游戏 Demo，取材《水浒传》第十回。草料场起了火，林冲提着花枪、挑着酒葫芦，穿过烧剩的草料场上山神庙；陆谦在庙前等着他。一个场景、两拨庄客、一场分两段的首领战，单机，电脑和手机都能玩。

Snow at the Mountain Temple is an independently developed top-down 3D action demo built with [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild), from chapter ten of *Water Margin*. The fodder yard is on fire; Lin Chong, spear in hand and wine gourd on its shaft, goes up through what is left of the yard to the Mountain God's temple, where Lu Qian is waiting. One place, two parties of hired men and a boss fight in two parts; single-player, on desktop and on phones.

[在线试玩 · Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/SnowTemple/)

## 玩法 · How to play

| | 键鼠 · Keyboard and mouse | 触屏 · Touch |
|---|---|---|
| 走 · Move | W A S D，或方向键 · W A S D or the arrow keys | 左侧拖动 · drag on the left |
| 出枪 · Strike | 左键 · left button | 大按钮 · the large button |
| 格挡 · Guard | 按住右键 · hold the right button | 按住 · hold |
| 闪避 · Dodge | 空格 · Space | 按钮 · button |
| 寒星 · Three Stars | F | 按钮 · button |
| 梨花枪雨 · Pear-Blossom Rain | Q | 按钮 · button |
| 踏雪 · Snow Step | 按住 Shift · hold Shift | 按住 · hold |
| 饮酒 · Drink | E | 按钮 · button |
| 行囊 · Bag | B | 点「行囊」 · tap the bag |

按键可以在「设置」里改。鼠标指向哪里，枪就朝哪里出；触屏时朝移动方向出，并自动转向近处的敌人。

Keys can be changed under Settings. With a mouse the spear goes where the pointer is; on glass it goes the way he is moving, turning to whoever is near.

- **三段连招**：刺、扫、劈，第三下是重击。出手时会转向瞄准线附近最近的人，并滑步到刚好够得着的距离。打中时双方各定格一瞬，其他人照常行动。
- **格挡与弹反**：按住格挡，正面的攻击按伤害扣气力，气力打空会破防。举枪后约四分之一秒内接住是弹反：不耗气力，对方长硬直。挡住之后脚下亮一圈金环，这时出枪是**回马枪**：抢到他身前，一枪重创。
- **闪避**能打断出招、施法、格挡和饮酒。在攻击里闪过去，时间会慢一瞬，并回一截气力。
- **打断**：在敌人抬手的后半段打中他，他这一招就废了，硬直更长，伤害更高。
- **寒星**：三道枪芒，会偏向前方的敌人，六秒一次。**梨花枪雨**：枪意攒满后，脚下开阵，二十六杆枪从天上落下来，收尾一震。
- **踏雪**：按住时贴着雪面疾行，耗气力，能穿过人群。
- 地上的红色预警是敌人要打的范围：橙红的可以挡，偏紫红的（砸地、箭雨、地火）挡不住，只能闪。
- **庄客**砍一刀，常常再补一刀。**牌手**会架开前两下轻击并立刻还手，第三下重击、寒星、绕后或弹反能破他。**弓手**拉满弓放箭，箭可以一枪挑落；每第三次改放箭雨。近战的同一时间只有一个上前，其余在外圈游走。
- **陆谦**使朴刀：横扫、二连、突刺、砸地。他有一条架势条，弹反和回马枪涨得最快，打满他会跪下一阵。血量过半他踢翻火盆，刀上带火，添了三路地火和两圈旋斩。
- 倒下的人会掉东西：铜钱、碎银、金创药（补一口酒，葫芦满时回血）、回气散（气力回满，一阵子回得更快）、枪谱残页（枪伤加一成，枪意加三成五）。走近自动拾取。
- **火盆**是歇脚处：烤一烤火，气血和酒葫芦都补满。倒下后从上一个火盆再来。

- **The three cuts**: a thrust, a sweep and an overhead blow, the third a heavy one. Each cut turns to the nearest man near the line of aim and slides in to just within reach. When a blow lands the two of them hold still for an instant while everyone else carries on.
- **Guard and parry**: hold to guard; blows from the front cost stamina by their weight, and an empty bar breaks the guard. Catch a blow within about a quarter of a second of raising the spear and it is a parry: no cost, and a long stagger for him. After any block a gold ring lights at his feet, and a strike then is the **riposte**: a step to the man's front and one heavy thrust.
- **The dodge** breaks out of cuts, casting, guarding and drinking. Dodge through a blow and the moment slows, and some stamina comes back.
- **Breaking an attack**: hit a man in the second half of his windup and the attack is spoiled, with a longer stagger and more damage.
- **Three Stars**: three bolts of spear light that lean toward whoever is ahead, every six seconds. **Pear-Blossom Rain**: with a full bar of resolve, a formation opens under him and twenty-six spears fall from the sky, ending in one burst.
- **Snow Step**: hold to skim over the snow; it costs stamina and passes through bodies.
- A red warning on the ground is where a blow will land: orange-red can be guarded, crimson (slams, volleys, ground fire) cannot and has to be dodged.
- **Blades** cut and often cut again. **Shield men** turn the first two light cuts aside and answer at once; the heavy third cut, the stars, getting behind or a parry opens them. **Bowmen** draw and loose arrows that can be cut out of the air, and every third time loose a volley. Only one man at a time comes in to strike; the rest walk the ring.
- **Lu Qian** carries a long broadsword: a sweep, two cuts, a lunge, a slam. He has a posture bar that parries and ripostes fill fastest; when it is full he kneels for a while. At half health he kicks over the brazier: the blade burns, and he adds three runs of ground fire and a double spin.
- The fallen drop things: coins, silver, salve (one more drink, or health when the gourd is full), tonic (stamina filled and quicker to return for a while) and a page of the spear manual (a tenth more damage and a third of a bar of resolve). They are picked up by walking near.
- **Braziers** are resting places: warming his hands fills health and gourd. After a fall he rises at the last one.

## 画面与技术 · Picture and technology

- 全部用引擎的 3D 模块（`@egret/engine/world3d`）和 WebGL 2 渲染器：带阴影的月光、最近的六盏灯火轮流点亮、泛光、环境光遮蔽、贴地的高度雾。
- 人物、兵器、石狮、香炉、山石、松树和草垛是**雕出来的**：`tools/sculpt.mjs` 把每个模型写成一组会互相融合的软体（胶囊、椭球、圆角盒），在细网格上求出表面，得到带平滑法线、颜色、褶缝阴影和骨骼权重的高面数网格（主角约十五万个三角面）。头、手和靴子各在更细的网格上单独雕：脸有眉骨、颧骨、带眼睑和瞳仁的眼睛、鼻翼和鼻孔、上下唇、耳廓；手有四根三节的手指、指节、指甲和拇指；靴子有踝骨、脚背、鞋头和鞋底，存进 `assets/models.bin`。五个人物共用一副十五根骨头的骨架，蒙皮绑定，姿势逐帧追赶。山门、庙和石灯仍是代码搭的。
- 五万多丛枯草是一次实例化绘制，顶点着色器里随阵风起伏，人走过会被拨开。
- 枪光、法阵、地面预警和光柱是「网格加一段片元着色器」画的：扫掠、噪声条纹和侵蚀都在着色器里；火星、雪尘和飘雪是实例化的光点。
- 规则（`src/rules`）与画面分开，不依赖浏览器，可以直接用 Node 测试；`test/bot.mjs` 是一个按规则出招的玩家，测试用它通关，地址加 `?auto` 可以看它打。
- 声音全部由振荡器和噪声合成：风声、五声音阶的拨弦、兵刃声。没有配音。
- 设置里可调画质（自动、低、中、高）、粒子数量、声音和全部按键；自动画质会按帧率升降绘制分辨率。

- Everything is drawn with the engine's 3D module (`@egret/engine/world3d`) and its WebGL 2 renderer: shadowed moonlight, the six nearest lamps lit at a time, bloom, ambient occlusion and low-lying height fog.
- The people, weapons, stone lions, censer, rocks, pines and fodder stacks are **sculpted**: `tools/sculpt.mjs` takes each model as a set of soft solids (capsules, eggs, rounded boxes) that melt into one another, finds the surface on a fine grid, and writes a dense mesh with smooth normals, colour, shadow in its creases and bone weights (about a hundred and fifty thousand triangles for the hero). Heads, hands and boots are each sculpted on a finer grid of their own: a face has a brow, cheekbones, eyes with lids and irises, a nose with wings and nostrils, two lips and ears; a hand has four fingers of three joints, knuckles, nails and a thumb; a boot has ankle bones, an instep, a toe and a sole into `assets/models.bin`. The five figures share one skeleton of fifteen bones and are skinned to it, each joint easing toward its pose every frame. The gate, the temple and the lanterns are still put together in code.
- More than fifty thousand tufts of dry grass are one instanced draw, swaying with gusts in the vertex shader and parting round whoever walks through.
- Spear light, the formation, the ground warnings and the columns of light are a mesh plus a fragment shader: the sweep, the grain and the erosion happen in the shader. Sparks, snow dust and falling snow are instanced dots.
- The rules (`src/rules`) are kept apart from the picture and do not need a browser, so Node tests them directly; `test/bot.mjs` is a player made of rules that the tests finish the chapter with, and `?auto` in the address lets it play.
- All sound is synthesized from oscillators and noise: wind, a plucked five-note tune, and the weapons. There is no voice acting.
- Settings cover picture quality (automatic, low, medium, high), particle amount, sound and every key; automatic quality moves the drawing resolution with the frame rate.

## 开发 · Development

```sh
node --test test/*.test.mjs      # 规则测试 · rule tests
node tools/models.mjs            # 重新雕刻全部模型（约两分钟），或只雕点名的：node tools/models.mjs hero lu · sculpt every model again (about two minutes), or only those named
python3 -m http.server 4180      # 模型查看器 · model viewer: /SnowTemple/tools/look.html?m=hero&yaw=0.4&y=1.6&d=0.8
# 在仓库根目录运行，然后打开 /SnowTemple/ · run at the repository root, then open /SnowTemple/
```

地址参数 · Address options: `?auto`（规则玩家代打 · a rule-driven player takes over），`?at=yard|gate|boss|rage`（从后面开始 · begin further in），`?quality=low|mid|high`，`?debug`（状态面板，F3 开关 · state panel, F3 toggles）。

| 路径 · Path | 内容 · Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/rules/` | 地形、数值与战斗规则 · the ground, the numbers and the rules of the fight |
| `src/view/` | 建模、场景、人物姿势、特效与镜头 · modelling, scenery, poses, effects and the camera |
| `src/hud.mjs` `src/input.mjs` `src/audio.mjs` | 界面、输入、声音 · interface, input, sound |
| `tools/` `assets/` | 雕刻工具、模型定义与雕好的模型 · the sculptor, the model descriptions and the sculpted models |
| `test/` | 规则测试与规则玩家 · rule tests and the rule-driven player |
| `vendor/` | 引擎运行时副本，见 `vendor/SOURCE.md` · a copy of the engine runtime, see `vendor/SOURCE.md` |

`vendor/` 中是新白鹭引擎的运行时副本，采用 Apache-2.0 许可，来源和版本见 [vendor/SOURCE.md](vendor/SOURCE.md)。

`vendor/` holds a copy of the Egret Rebuild runtime under the Apache-2.0 license; its source and version are in [vendor/SOURCE.md](vendor/SOURCE.md).
