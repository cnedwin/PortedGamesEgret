# 桃溪县令 · Magistrate of Peach Creek

## 简介 · Introduction

桃溪县令是基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)独立开发的游戏 Demo：一款宋代背景、画风治愈的放置经营游戏。你从桃溪下县的县令做起，规划坊区、修屋开田、通市开窑、勘探山林矿石、访贤派驻、整修城防，一步步把小县升格到「州」。离开时县城照常运转，回来时清点收成。一任至少要玩三天，升迁后还有更难的下一任。

Magistrate of Peach Creek is a game demo made on its own on [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild), the
new Egret engine: a gentle idle city-builder set in the Song dynasty. You start as magistrate of a lesser county,
plan the wards, raise houses and fields, open markets and kilns, survey the hills for timber, stone, clay and iron,
call on worthies and post them, keep the walls, and lift the county rank by rank to a prefecture. The county goes on
while you are away and counts the harvest when you return. A term takes three days or more, and promotion brings a
harder one.

[在线试玩 · Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/PeachCreek/)

## 玩法与操作 · How to play

- **坊区与选址**：十二个坊共 144 块地，开局开放前三坊。在地图或「扩建」中选相连的新坊，自行决定扩建方向。近县衙适合开市，城边适合耕作，民居可建在任意开放地块。坊区规划提供加成，可以随时调整。长距离运输增加运费，烟尘会拖累邻近民居。 · **Wards and siting**: twelve wards hold 144 plots, with three open at first. Choose a neighboring ward to expand from the map or the expansion ledger. Trade benefits from being near the yamen, fields favor the outskirts, and homes can go on any open plot. Plans grant a bonus and can be changed. Long hauls increase costs and smoke hurts nearby homes.
- **营建**：空地上选建筑，点已有建筑可比较升级前后的样式。建筑每级都有细节变化，重要等级会扩展整体轮廓。等级到 10、25、50、75 时产出翻倍。 · **Building**: choose what to build on a free plot, or select an existing building to compare its current and next appearance. Each level changes the details, and milestones change the silhouette. Output doubles at levels 10, 25, 50 and 75.
- **产业链**：稻田产粮，粮酿酒；桑园产桑，桑织绸；山里的陶土和木柴烧瓷；茶园产茶。集市和码头把货卖成钱，但不卖县衙升格要用的货。 · **Chains**: paddies give grain, grain makes wine; mulberry makes silk; clay and wood fire porcelain; gardens grow tea. Markets and the dock sell goods for coin, but never what the yamen's next rise needs.
- **特殊选址**：码头只能建在两个南岸坊的蓝色临水地块，工程簿可跳到这些地块。旧档里的陆上码头可以免费迁到水岸，保留等级、工程和人才。资源点按地形勘探开发。 · **Special sites**: docks require a blue waterfront plot in one of the two south-bank wards. Find them in the Works sheet. Old inland docks can move there for free and retain levels, work and talent. Survey the county for resource deposits.
- **资源点**：县城北面山里有十二处，按顺序勘探，找到后开发成林场、采石场、陶土坑和铁矿。 · **Resource points**: twelve in the hills to the north, surveyed in turn and worked as woods, quarries, clay pits and iron mines.
- **民生与人口**：民居给人住，医馆、书院、寺庙、戏楼、园林添民生；民生越高，人来得越快、税收越多。窑和酒坊有烟，太多会拉低民生。 · **People and livelihood**: houses give room; clinics, schools, temples, theatres and gardens add livelihood, which brings people faster and more tax. Kilns and breweries smoke, and too much smoke lowers it.
- **街巷**：人口增加后，街上会出现更多居民；农作、商铺、工坊和驻防会带来不同装束和用具的人物。 · **Street life**: as the population grows, more residents take to the streets. Farms, shops, workshops and garrisons bring workers with their own clothes and tools.
- **县衙**：税银攒在县衙，点一下收进库（离开时最多攒几小时）。人口、民生和货物够了就能升格，升格需要几分钟到半天，并开放新建筑、更高等级和更多工匠。 · **The yamen**: taxes wait there until tapped (up to a few hours while away). With enough people, livelihood and goods it can rise a rank, which takes from minutes to half a day and opens buildings, higher levels and more builders.
- **人才**：花声望访贤，二十位人才分吏、才、贤、杰四等，每 8 小时一次免费，最多 25 次之内必出贤才或杰才。人才可以派驻到建筑、资源点、城防，或任县丞、主簿、县尉，各看治、农、工、商、武一项；派驻的时间越长等级越高。 · **Talents**: fame calls on worthies — twenty of them in four ranks, a free call every eight hours, and a worthy or better within any 25 calls. Post them to buildings, resource points, the defences or the three offices, each reading one of rule, farming, craft, trade and arms; they grow while posted.
- **城防**：四面城墙和门楼可以分别升格。升到上县后可调遣小队，乡勇营扩充预备兵力。来袭方向在警报中标出，守军、门楼和城墙按该方向计算战力。布防也能减轻守城失利的损失。 · **Defences**: upgrade each of the four walls and gatehouses separately. Squads can be assigned from upper-county rank, and the militia camp expands the reserve. The raid alert names the threatened approach. Its wall, gatehouse and squads determine defence and can limit losses.
- **民情**：上任约五分钟可处理第一件民情。之后按时节、县等抽取新事件，一次处理一件。作决定前可查看花费、收益和持续时间。 · **Petitions**: the first arrives about five minutes into office. Later cases depend on season and county rank, with one at a time. Each reply lists its cost, reward and any timed effects.
- **季节和昼夜**：每 30 分钟换一季。春宜拓田、夏宜茶桑、秋宜储粮经商，冬季酒坊、织坊和瓷窑产量提高。点击上方节候查看各项变化和换季时间，入夜按本机时间点灯。 · **Seasons and night**: seasons turn every thirty minutes. Open paddies in spring, grow tea and mulberry in summer, store grain and sell goods in autumn, and run workshops in winter. The almanac shows details and the time to the next season. Lanterns light by your clock.

## 竞品调研 · What similar games taught us

| 参考 · Reference | 学到的 · What we took | 没学的 · What we left |
|---|---|---|
| 《江南百景图》 · Jiangnan Hundred Scenes | 古城分区布局、人物有属性派驻到建筑、平和的画风 · zoned old-town layout, characters with stats posted to buildings, a calm look | 桃溪的选址以地块、运输与邻里为主 · Peach Creek uses parcels, hauling and neighboring uses |
| 《这城有良田》等县令题材放置游戏 · county-magistrate idle games | 县令一级级升官的主线、访贤有稀有度和保底、民情二选一 · the climb through ranks, calls with rarity and pity, two-way petitions | 大量付费加速和体力，本作没有任何付费 · paid speed-ups and stamina; there is nothing to buy here |
| AdVenture Capitalist、Idle Miner 一类放置游戏 · idle tycoons | 等级里程碑产出翻倍（10/25/50/75）、离线收益有上限且能靠建筑提高（仓廪） · output doubling at level milestones, capped offline earnings raised by buildings (granaries) | 无限重置和指数爆炸的数字，改成一任有终点、升迁后要求提高 · endless resets and runaway numbers; a term has an end, and promotion asks for more |
| Clash of Clans 一类城建游戏 · base builders | 工匠是并行的工位、升级有真实工期、主建筑（县衙）卡住最高等级 · builders as parallel slots, real-time upgrades, the main building capping levels | 攻打别人的城，本作只守不攻 · attacking others; here the town only defends |
| 古风治愈放置游戏 · gentle old-style idle games | 四季、昼夜、街上走动的小人和溪上的小船让城看起来是活的 · seasons, night, people and boats that make the town feel alive | 社交和排行 · social features and ladders |

由此定下的节奏原则：开局十分钟内要有「第一次升格」；每次回来都有可收的税、可领的目标或完工的建筑；县衙每次升格是一个台阶，要攒人口、民生和一两种新货，同时解锁新东西；离开再久，也只按仓廪允许的几个小时算收成，所以常来看看的人走得快，但偶尔来的人也不会被甩得太远。

The pacing rules that came from this: the first rise within ten minutes; something to collect, claim or see finished
on every return; each rise of the yamen a step that asks for people, livelihood and one or two new goods, and opens
something new; and an absence counted only for the hours the granaries allow, so frequent visitors go faster without
leaving the occasional one far behind.

Start with 600 coin, 300 grain, 200 wood and 30 people. Each person eats half a grain an hour and pays
tax according to livelihood. The yamen also pays a stipend. Hauling and smoke matter more at higher ranks.

## 已知问题 · Known issues

- 横屏触摸流程已用桌面浏览器的触控模拟验收。手机实机帧时间与扬声器听感仍待验收。 · A desktop browser's landscape touch emulation covered the touch flow. Phone frame pacing and speaker listening still need physical-device checks.

## 已解决 · Solved

- 模拟玩家把市坊修满了医馆、卡在没有绸：建筑优先修在自己的坊并限制同类数量，产业链提前一级解锁，集市留下升格要用的货。 · The simulated player filled the market ward with clinics and stalled without silk: buildings now prefer their own ward with a cap per kind, chains open a rank earlier, and markets keep what the next rise needs.
- 来敌比城防长得快：来敌战力改为跟着县等和人口走，城防等级的里程碑也翻倍。 · Raids outgrew defences: raid strength now follows rank and people, and defence levels get the milestone doubling too.
- 开局缺钱：开局给一座两级民居、一块稻田和一处林场，县衙另发俸禄。 · Coin ran dry at the start: the county begins with a house, a paddy and a woodland, and the yamen pays a salary.
- 第二任太快（勤快玩家 2.5 天，因为人才留下了）：每任要求的增幅从 45 % 提到 80 %。 · The second term was too quick (2.5 days for the keen player, the talents having stayed): each term now asks 80 % more instead of 45 %.
- 升级中的建筑被整块蒙住，满城像没建好：只有新建才蒙住，升级改为在一侧搭竹架、插红旗。 · Buildings being raised were veiled and the town looked unbuilt: only new buildings are veiled now; a raise shows a bamboo frame and a red pennant at the side.
- 引擎方面见 [ENGINE-NOTES.md](ENGINE-NOTES.md)：Tab 和 Space 被 `play` 拿走（第 1 条）、透明画布下的黑盒子（第 2 条）、画布分辨率（第 3 条）、存档回调的返回值（第 4 条）、`game.rect` 改不了颜色（第 5 条）、`game.burst` 用世界坐标（第 6 条），都已绕过。 · On the engine side see ENGINE-NOTES.md, items 1 to 6, all worked round.

## 未完成 · Unfinished

- 建筑之间的相邻加成（例如书院挨着园林）只做到「同类坊区加成」一层。 · Adjacency between buildings (a school beside a garden) goes no further than the ward bonus.
- 人才只有派驻和成长，没有人才之间的羁绊或专属事件。 · Talents are posted and grow, with no bonds or events of their own.
- 历任记录只记任数和最快用时，没有每一任的城市留影。 · The record keeps the number of terms and the fastest, not a picture of each town.
