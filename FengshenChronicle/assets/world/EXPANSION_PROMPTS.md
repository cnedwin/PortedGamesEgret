# RPG town and outpost expansion

Three original assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, without reference inputs. Original PNGs remain outside the project. Delivered WebP assets preserve dimensions and original alpha exactly. Source image hashes and converted file hashes are in `SOURCES.json`.

Consistent orthographic three-quarter top-down front view, horizontal roof ridges and south-facing fronts, verticals vertical, no diamond isometric. TOP MIDDLE: a prosperous rustic inn/tavern, weathered dark timber, warm red cloth awning, hanging round lanterns, stacked wine jars, inviting doorway and raised stone threshold, no lettering. TOP RIGHT: a dignified ancestral ritual shrine, low sloping black-tile roof with archaic bronze ridge ornaments, vermilion columns, bronze incense vessel within its stair footprint, subdued warm lamps, no readable symbols. BOTTOM LEFT: a compact fortified frontier watchpost, square rammed-earth and timber base, elevated open lookout platform with a simple timber roof, bronze shield mounts and a crimson battle pennant, no people. BOTTOM MIDDLE: a broad military barracks building with stout carved beams, charcoal tiled roof, red and ochre standards, bronze-studded double doors and stone stoop. BOTTOM RIGHT: an organic small tree cluster: one branching ginkgo tree with golden-green individual leaves, a slender graceful green pine and low shrubs, clear roots and foliage detail rather than oval shapes. Rich hand-painted textures, restrained soft attached foot shadows. Transparent background, NO backdrop or ground plane, NO people, NO modern objects, NO text, NO labels, NO UI. Whole assets uncropped and separate.

## Runtime use

Storefronts replace existing city building artwork at the same colliders and foot pivots. New military buildings use only the rule-owned `pass` obstacles. Animal frames are rasterized once into the shared atlas; their short presentation-only routes are defined by `src/art/ambient.mjs`. The animals have no combat, interaction or collision state. Ambient animation can be disabled or reduced without changing gameplay.
