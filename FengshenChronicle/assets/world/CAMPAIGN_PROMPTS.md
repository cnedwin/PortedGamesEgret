## Preparation and scope

All source pages are cached into native Egret atlases at startup. New class pages use connected alpha silhouettes and measured contact pixels. Weapon, hand and boot anchors share the same pose. Expensive cutout and atlas work yields to the browser between batches; pure prepared silhouettes are reused on orientation rebuild, while each game gets fresh atlas entries. Six map textures and six atlas pages currently load before play; no work was deferred to the new-game button. Image source pages never become extra fullscreen runtime layers. River bridge paving spans the exact 280px passable gap; railings remain outside it. Filled warnings stay on the ground; a thin unfilled outline and safe-zone edge remain visible over roof overhangs.
