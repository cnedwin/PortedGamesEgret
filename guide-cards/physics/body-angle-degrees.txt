# A 2D physics body's angle is in degrees

`PhysicsBodyOptions.angle` and `PhysicsBody.angle` are degrees, as a display object's `rotation` is. Given radians, a sloped platform turns by less than a degree: a wheel driven toward it meets the end face of a box that never turned and stops dead, which looks like a fault in the contacts.

```js
// A kinematic plank whose top edge runs from (ax, ay) to (bx, by).
plank.angle = (Math.atan2(by - ay, bx - ax) * 180) / Math.PI;
```

Rules:

- Convert once where the angle is set; keep radians in your own maths.
- The same holds for debris you spin by hand: add degrees a step.
- Bodies do not turn by themselves yet (no angular speed or torque): a wheel slides, so drive it along the surface it stands on.
