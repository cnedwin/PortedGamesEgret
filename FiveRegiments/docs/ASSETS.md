# 帝国竞技场 · 素材来源 · Imperial Arena · Where the assets come from

本作没有使用任何现成游戏的美术。人物、战马和场景物件由 Tripo 按文字描述生成，动作来自公开的动作捕捉库，天空和材质是 CC0 素材，兵器、特效、界面和声音都由代码生成。

No art from any existing game is used. The men, the horse and the props were generated by Tripo from written descriptions, the
motion comes from a public motion-capture library, the skies and materials are CC0, and the weapons, effects,
interface and sound are made in code.

## 人物、战马与场景物件 · The men, the horse and the props

- 生成：[Tripo](https://www.tripo3d.ai/)（tripo-cli 0.11，模型 `tripo-v3.1`，PBR 材质，贴图质量 detailed）。人物另加几何质量 detailed，并用 `rig-v3.0` 绑定到虚幻引擎风格的人形骨架（`spec=unreal`）；战马用 `rig-v2.5` 按四足动物绑定（`rig_type=quadruped`），另带一段 `preset:quadruped:walk` 的走路动画（游戏里的步态是代码驱动的，这段动画只在开发时对照）。每个人物 75 点、战马 85 点、每件物件 30 点，共约 1420 点。使用条款见 [Tripo Terms of Service](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)。 · Generated with Tripo (`tripo-v3.1`, PBR, detailed textures; the men also detailed geometry and rigged with `rig-v3.0` to an Unreal-style humanoid skeleton; the horse rigged with `rig-v2.5` as a quadruped, with a `preset:quadruped:walk` clip kept only for comparison, the game's gaits being driven in code). About 1,420 credits in all. Terms: the Tripo Terms of Service.
- 文字描述：人物和战马见 [`tools/assets/characters.tsv`](../tools/assets/characters.tsv)（五营各一，统一要求 T 姿势、空手；战马披铠带鞍、无骑手），物件见 [`tools/assets/props.tsv`](../tools/assets/props.tsv)（三十二件）。 · The descriptions are in `tools/assets/characters.tsv` (one a regiment in a T-pose with empty hands, and the horse in barding and saddle without a rider) and `tools/assets/props.tsv` (thirty-two props).
- 处理：[`tools/assets/pack.mjs`](../tools/assets/pack.mjs)（人物）和 [`tools/assets/packprop.mjs`](../tools/assets/packprop.mjs)（物件）用 [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev/) 读模型、[sharp](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/) 把贴图缩小并重新压成 JPEG，再用 [gltfpack](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer)（meshoptimizer）减面、量化、meshopt 压缩，分高、中、低三档。人物三档是 4.2 万 / 1.8 万 / 7 千个三角形，贴图 2048 / 1024 / 512。 · Packed by those two scripts: glTF-Transform to read, sharp to resize and re-encode the textures as JPEG, gltfpack to simplify, quantize and compress, in high, medium and low sets (the men at 42,000 / 18,000 / 7,000 triangles and 2048 / 1024 / 512 textures).
- 打包时不用 gltfpack 的激进简化（`-sa`）：它会合并贴图接缝，中、低两档的贴图会被拉花。 · The packing does not use gltfpack's aggressive simplification (`-sa`), which merges texture seams and smears the medium and low sets.
- 打包脚本需要的依赖：`npm i @gltf-transform/core @gltf-transform/extensions meshoptimizer sharp gltfpack`，在装好依赖的目录里运行：`node pack.mjs <model.glb> <out dir> <name>`、`node packprop.mjs <model.glb> <out dir> <name> <high tris> <mid tris> <low tris> <high texture size>`。 · The scripts need those packages and run as shown.

## 动作 · Motion

- 来源：卡内基梅隆大学图形实验室动作捕捉库 [CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/)。使用的是转成 GLB 的版本 [codepawl/cmu-mocap-glb](https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/cmu-mocap-glb)。按该库的[说明](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php)，数据可以自由使用（包括商业用途），不可原样转售数据本身。 · From the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, in the GLB conversion codepawl/cmu-mocap-glb. Per the database's FAQ the data may be used freely, commercially included, but not resold as raw data.
- 致谢原文 · Credit, as the database asks: *The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.*
- 用到的片段（[`tools/assets/clips.json`](../tools/assets/clips.json)）：站立 140_06（第 536–666 帧）、走 02_01（60–124）、跑 16_35（18–66）、后退 111_01（52–130）、侧移 113_18（252–328，另一侧为镜像）。 · Clips used: idle 140_06, walk 02_01, run 16_35, back 111_01, side step 113_18 (mirrored for the other side).
- 处理：[`tools/retarget.mjs`](../tools/retarget.mjs) 离线重定向到每个人物的骨架，输出 `assets/motion/<营>.json` 和 `.bin`。 · Retargeted offline by `tools/retarget.mjs` into `assets/motion/<regiment>.json` and `.bin`.
- 出手、格挡、踢、拉弓、技能、受击、倒地不是动作捕捉，是 `src/view/figure.mjs` 里按兵器写的姿态。 · Blows, guards, kicks, the bow, skills, hits and falls are not captured; they are poses written in `src/view/figure.mjs`.

## 天空与材质 · Skies and materials

全部来自 [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)，[CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) 许可（公有领域，无需署名，这里仍列出）。[`tools/assets/textures.mjs`](../tools/assets/textures.mjs) 把 HDRI 色调映射成 2048×1024 的 JPEG 全景图，材质的颜色和法线缩到 1024、AO/粗糙度/金属度缩到 512。

All from Poly Haven under CC0 (public domain; listed anyway). `tools/assets/textures.mjs` tone-maps the HDRIs to
2048 × 1024 JPEG panoramas and resizes the material maps (colour and normal 1024, AO/roughness/metal 512).

## 代码生成的部分 · Made in code
