# 九州烽烟 · Nine Provinces Ablaze

## 简介 · Introduction

九州烽烟是基于[新白鹭引擎 Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild)独立开发的游戏 Demo：一款东汉末年到三国归晋的即时制大战略游戏。从中平元年（184 年）黄巾起事开始，你执掌三十四路诸侯之一，在七十六座城、十一处关隘、三十四个渡口连成的天下图上经营城池、招揽武将、研究科技、合纵连横；军团在地图上按天行军，两军相遇或攻城时进入六角格即时战场，由你排兵布阵、下令、放战法。六条演义（曹操、刘备、孙氏、董卓、袁绍和自由书写）各有剧情事件与抉择，一直走到太康元年（280 年）。

Nine Provinces Ablaze is a game demo made on its own on [Egret Rebuild](https://github.com/cnedwin/Egret_rebuild), the
new Egret engine: a real-time grand strategy game from the last years of the Han to the Jin. It opens in 184 with
the Yellow Turbans; you lead one of thirty-four lords on a map of seventy-six cities, eleven passes and thirty-four
ferries, running cities, winning officers, studying inventions and making and breaking alliances. Armies march on the
map day by day; when two meet, or a city is stormed, the battle moves to a field of hexes in real time, where you
deploy, give orders and unleash tactics. Six tales (Cao Cao, Liu Bei, the Suns, Dong Zhuo, Yuan Shao, and one of your
own) each have events and choices of their own, and the age runs on to 280.

[在线试玩 · Play online](https://cnedwin.github.io/PortedGamesEgret/NineProvinces/)

## 玩法与操作 · How to play

标题页选「新的征程」：先选演义（五条史实演义各自固定势力，「自由书写」可选任何一路诸侯），再定规则——寿命按史实或虚构、战斗亲自指挥或全部委任、难度。开局后时间自动流逝，顶栏可暂停和调五档速度（每秒半天到八天）。 · On the title choose New campaign: first a tale (the five tales of history each come with their power; Write Your Own lets you take any lord), then the rules — lifespans as in history or made up, battles under your command or left to your officers, and the difficulty. Time then runs on its own; the top bar pauses it and sets five speeds (half a day to eight days a second).

- **天下图**：七十六城、十一关、三十四津，陆路、河道、海路相连。各家疆域按城的归属着色，城上插着城主的旗号；行军中的军团、被围的城都看得见。点城或军团看详情，鼠标停留看提示。 · **The realm**: seventy-six cities, eleven passes and thirty-four ferries joined by roads, rivers and the sea. Each power's land is coloured from the places it holds, each city flies its lord's banner, and armies on the march and cities under siege show on the map. Tap a place or an army for its details; rest the mouse on it for a note.
- **内政**：每城有人口、民心、治安、守军和训练，四项营建（农田、市场、城墙、官署）各有上限。每月一号结算钱粮，八月秋收；税率轻重影响民心。征兵每月以人口的二十分之一为限，会降低民心和训练。太守由政治和统率最高的武将担任，影响营建、产出与征兵。 · **Running the realm**: each city has people, support, order, a garrison and its training, and four works (fields, markets, walls, offices) up to their limits. Coin and grain come in on the first of each month, with the harvest in the eighth; taxes trade coin for support. Conscription is limited to a twentieth of the people each month and lowers support and training. The best administrator posted there governs, and raises works, income and recruits.
- **武将**：五百六十六位史实人物（统率、武力、智力、政治、魅力五项，二百一十三种特性，一百三十二个专属战法，一百零八件宝物），按史实年份成年登场、按寿命谢世；每年还有新人出仕或在野。派武将寻访本城的在野人才和宝物，登用在野、俘虏或敌将，颁布招贤令吸引附近的人才。 · **Officers**: five hundred and sixty-six figures of history (command, war, intellect, politics and charm; two hundred and thirteen traits; one hundred and thirty-two signature tactics; one hundred and eight treasures), coming of age in their own years and dying in theirs; each year new men take service or wait unattached. Send officers to search a city for talent and treasures, persuade the unattached, captives and enemy officers, and issue the order for talent to draw in those nearby.
- **出征**：选主将和最多两名副将、兵力、二十六种兵种（枪、戟、刀盾、弓、弩、骑、器械、水军，以及虎豹骑、白马义从、陷阵营、藤甲兵、象兵等专属兵种）和八种阵型，再在地图上点目的地。敌城会被围攻，守军断粮后每天减员；军粮随军，在己方城池补给。 · **War**: choose a commander and up to two deputies, the troops, one of twenty-six kinds (spears, halberds, shields, bows, crossbows, horse, engines, ships, and corps of their own such as the Tiger and Leopard Riders, the White Horse Volunteers, the Formation Breakers, rattan armour and war elephants) and one of eight formations, then tap the destination on the map. Enemy places are besieged, and a garrison cut off loses men each day; an army carries its grain and takes on more in friendly places.
- **战场**：二十二乘十四的六角格，地形有平原、草地、树林、山地、浅滩、江面、沼泽、城墙、城门、城内、营寨和战船。枪克骑、骑克弓、弓克戟、戟克枪；攻城要撞开城门或占据本营，守方坚持到日落即胜。怒气满时放战法——直线、周围一圈或两圈、自身或身边友军；火计会点燃树林和战船，顺风蔓延。乌巢夜袭与赤壁火攻是两场特别的战斗。 · **The field**: twenty-two by fourteen hexes of plain, grass, woods, hills, shallows, open water, marsh, walls, gates, town, camps and moored ships. Spears beat horse, horse beats bows, bows beat halberds, halberds beat spears; a siege is won by breaking the gate or holding the keep, and the defenders win by lasting till sunset. With full rage a general unleashes a tactic — along a line, round the target in one ring or two, on himself or on allies near him; fire sets woods and ships alight and spreads with the wind. The night raid on Wuchao and the fire at the Red Cliffs are battles of their own.
- **科技**：三十三项，分根基、霸道、王道三路，五层；第四、五层两条路线互相排斥。 · **Inventions**: thirty-three in three ways (foundations, dominance, benevolence) and five tiers; the fourth and fifth tiers of the two ways shut each other out.
- **外交**：好感、赠礼、同盟（两年）、停战（一年）、毁约、劝降、请盟友出兵；电脑君主也会结盟、遣使，并联手对付独大的一方。 · **Diplomacy**: regard, gifts, alliances (two years), truces (a year), breaking them, demanding submission and asking an ally to march; the computer's lords make alliances, send envoys and turn together on any power grown too great.
- **演义**：四十一个事件，二十三个由你抉择（十常侍、董卓入京、讨董联军、迁都、连环计、桃园结义、三顾茅庐、官渡、赤壁、白衣渡江……）；结局有一统天下、鼎足一方、存续至终和败亡。 · **The tale**: forty-one events, twenty-three of them yours to decide (the Ten Attendants, Dong Zhuo in the capital, the coalition, the move to Chang'an, the chain stratagem, the peach garden, the thrice-visited cottage, Guandu, the Red Cliffs, the crossing in white…); the endings are the realm united, one leg of the tripod, enduring to the end, and the fall.

开局各城钱粮按势力大小给，电脑在难度「难」时多三成。电脑在开局前三百天（易 540 天、难 120 天）不打玩家；在史实演义里，汉室、丁原、董卓等要在剧情里出场的势力在各自的事件之前不被电脑吞并。百年推演一天约 2 毫秒（Node）。 · Each power starts with coin and grain by its size, and the computer's with a third more on hard. A century takes about 2 ms a day in Node.

## 已知问题 · Known issues

- 帧时间只在软件渲染下测过：天下图暂停时约 32 ms 一帧，主要是引擎画布每帧整屏合成；有显卡的机器和手机实机未运行。 · Frame times were measured only under software rendering, where the paused map takes about 32 ms a frame, mostly the engine canvas composited whole each frame; machines with a GPU and real phones were not run.
- 拖动或缩放天下图时，网页画布要按新视野重画整张底图（软件渲染下约 37 ms）。 · Dragging or zooming the map repaints the whole page canvas for the new view (about 37 ms under software rendering).
- 战场在窄屏上较小，两侧的部队牌占去一部分宽度；可以滚轮或双指放大。 · On narrow screens the field is small, the unit cards taking part of the width; zoom with the wheel or two fingers.
- 舞台的横竖在载入时按窗口定下（横 1600×900、竖 900×1600），之后转动手机不会重建，画面按 `cover` 裁切；重新载入即可。 · The stage's shape is fixed by the window when the page loads (1600 × 900 wide, 900 × 1600 tall); turning a phone afterwards does not rebuild it, and the picture is cropped by `cover` until a reload.
- 只在无头环境里听过合成的波形，没有用扬声器听过。 · The synthesized sound was checked as waveforms only, never through a speaker.
- 连环计等少数史实事件要求的人物若先在战斗中阵亡或被俘，事件就会错过。 · A few events of history, such as the chain stratagem, are missed when the men they need have already fallen or been taken in battle.
- 平衡只靠电脑对电脑的百年推演调过，没有真人玩满一局的记录。 · The balance was tuned only by the computer playing itself for a century; no person has played a whole game.

## 已解决 · Solved

- 一百多个地名用引擎文字标签会超出每帧图片页数上限：地名、城的图标和旗号改画在网页画布上，军团和部队的旗字改用一张共用的字符图集。 · A hundred place names as engine labels went past the per-frame limit on image pages: names, city marks and flags are painted on the page canvas, and the characters on banners come from one shared atlas.
- 软件渲染下引擎整屏画地图太慢：静止的山川疆域画在网页画布上，引擎只画会动的；远景时改用半尺寸的底图。 · Drawing the whole map in the engine was too slow under software rendering: the still land is painted on the page canvas and the engine draws what moves; far out, a half-size copy of the land is drawn.
- 同一种子两局结果不同：战场上缓存的邻格列表被排序改动了；改为复制后再排序。 · One seed gave two histories: a cached list of neighbouring hexes was sorted in place; it is copied before sorting now.
- 战斗太慢（开局一年每天 16 ms）：六角格距离改为预先算好的表，寻路改用定长数组，武将名单只列一次。 · Battles were slow (16 ms a day in the first year): hex distances come from a table worked out once, pathfinding uses fixed arrays, and the roll of officers is listed once.
- 攻城时守军只守城墙，敌军进了城门直接占了本营：城破后守军回防本营。 · Defenders kept to the walls while the enemy walked through the gate to the keep: once the walls are breached they turn to the keep.
- 新人生成后旧局的新人留在名册里：读档或开新局时先清掉别局的新人。 · Officers made in one game stayed in the roll for the next: they are cleared before a game is loaded or begun.
- 玩家开局几个月就被邻居吞并、史实势力在事件前就灭亡、荀彧和诸葛亮被电脑先登用：开局宽限期、史实势力在事件前受保护、演义要用的人物留给玩家、何进等人的寿终推迟到各自事件之后。 · The player was swallowed by a neighbour within months, powers of history fell before their events, and Xun Yu and Zhuge Liang were taken by the computer: a grace at the start, protection until the events, officers kept for the player's tale, and the deaths of He Jin and others put off until their events.
- 后期大国囤积上百万钱粮和兵力却不出兵：电脑的兵力按城数设上限，出兵时一次派足够的军团，围城时让守军断粮减员、有攻城器械或兵力足够时才强攻。 · Late in the age great powers hoarded millions and never marched: the computer's troops are capped by its cities, a campaign sends enough armies, and sieges starve the garrison and storm only with engines or enough men.
- 标题页一直在重画：标题改成静止画面，地图停稳后引擎停止绘制，开局时再启动。 · The title kept redrawing: it is a still picture now, the engine stopping once the map has settled and starting again with the game.
